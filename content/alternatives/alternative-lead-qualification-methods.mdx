# Alternative Lead Qualification Methods

There are several ways to approach lead qualification beyond the traditional manual process. Let's explore the main alternatives.

## Manual SDR Qualification

### How It Works
A sales development representative manually reviews each lead and decides if it meets criteria.

### Pros
- Personalized, human judgment
- Can handle complex scenarios
- Builds relationships

### Cons
- Expensive (SDR salaries)
- Slow (delays in reaching out)
- Inconsistent (varies by person)
- Doesn't scale well

### Cost: $40-60K annually per SDR

## Automated Rule-Based Systems

### How It Works
Leads are automatically routed based on predefined rules (industry, company size, etc.).

### Pros
- Fast and consistent
- Cheap to implement
- Scales infinitely

### Cons
- Rigid and inflexible
- Can't understand context
- High false positive rate

### Cost: $500-2000/month

## AI-Powered Voice Qualification

### How It Works
AI makes outbound calls, qualifies leads through natural conversation, and logs results.

### Pros
- Natural, engaging conversations
- 40% higher conversion rates
- Fast response time (seconds)
- Infinitely scalable
- Human-like follow-up

### Cons
- Requires API integration
- Learning curve for optimization

### Cost: $200-1000/month + per-lead fees

## Hybrid Approach

### How It Works
Combine AI qualification with human verification on edge cases.

### Pros
- Maintains human oversight
- Captures edge cases
- Optimal efficiency

### Cons
- More complex to manage
- Ongoing human involvement

### Cost: Varies

## Which Should You Choose?

- **Bootstrap stage**: Start with manual SDR qualification or rules
- **Growth stage**: Switch to hybrid (SDR + AI)
- **Scale stage**: Go full AI with Callbox

The goal is to minimize cost per qualified lead while maximizing quality as you grow.
