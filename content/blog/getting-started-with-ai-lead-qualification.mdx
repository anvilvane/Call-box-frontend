# Getting Started with AI Lead Qualification

AI-powered lead qualification is transforming how sales teams operate. In this guide, we'll walk you through the fundamentals and best practices.

## Why AI Lead Qualification Matters

Traditional lead qualification is manual, time-consuming, and prone to human error. An AI system can:

- Instantly qualify every inbound lead
- Maintain consistency across all interactions
- Free up your SDRs to focus on high-value activities
- Scale your qualification process without scaling your team

## Setting Up Your System

The first step is integrating your lead source with the AI qualification system. Here's what you need:

1. **API credentials** from your CRM or form platform
2. **Qualification criteria** specific to your business
3. **Routing rules** for different lead types

## Common Challenges and Solutions

Many teams struggle with:

- **Over-qualification**: Being too strict and missing good leads
- **Under-qualification**: Letting too many poor leads through
- **Inconsistent criteria**: Different team members having different standards

The solution is to periodically review your criteria and adjust based on conversion data.

## Next Steps

Once you've set up the basics, consider:
- A/B testing different qualification approaches
- Integrating with your email and SMS platforms
- Setting up real-time notifications for hot leads

Get started today and see the difference AI-powered qualification can make for your sales team!
